Legislative Framework – GDPR Compliance

The EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR, EU 2016/679) came into effect across the European Union on 25 May 2018, representing the most significant update to data protection law in two decades. Designed on principles of privacy by design and a risk-based approach, the GDPR establishes a modern framework for the protection of personal data in the digital age.

With the increasing use of technology, broader definitions of personal data, and significant growth in cross-border data processing, the GDPR aims to standardise data protection laws across the EU, granting individuals stronger and more consistent rights to access, control, and protect their personal information.

The purpose of this Cookie Policy (“Policy”) is to outline the measures that Luna Wealth Asset Management Ltd (LWAM) has implemented to comply with GDPR obligations and to demonstrate our commitment to safeguarding the privacy of all current, former, and prospective clients and website users.

This Policy provides detailed information on how LWAM collects, processes, and protects information obtained through this website (the “Site”), in full compliance with applicable EU and Cypriot data protection requirements.

What Is a Cookie

A cookie is a small text file placed on your computer, smartphone, or other device by the websites you visit or by certain emails you open. Cookies are commonly used to enhance your user experience, enable website functionality, maintain security, and provide the business with aggregated marketing and analytics information about website visitors.

Cookies consist of a combination of letters and numbers and are stored in different locations depending on their type:

  • Session cookies are temporarily stored in your device’s memory and are deleted when you close your browser.
  • Persistent cookies are stored on your device’s hard drive and remain there until they expire or are manually deleted.

This Cookie Policy provides information about the cookies used on the websites of Luna Wealth Asset Management Ltd, their purposes, and how you can reject or delete cookies if you prefer not to accept them.
