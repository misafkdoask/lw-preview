Article summaries

  1. 1

    Global finance risk and trade vulnerability

    A recent report from the United Nations Conference on Trade and Development highlights how weaknesses in international financial systems could threaten global trade and economic growth. It warns that unpredictable capital flows and financial stress disproportionately impact developing nations, undermining their ability to engage in stable trade. The report calls for reforms to make financial systems more predictable and resilient, which could help reduce vulnerability for emerging markets while supporting global economic stability.

  2. 2

    Weekly economics roundup from Deloitte

    Deloitte’s latest weekly economics brief pulls together the most notable developments affecting the global economy. It covers shifting central bank policies, market performance indicators, and emerging geopolitical risks that are shaping investor sentiment and macro-economic forecasts. The update is especially useful for tracking how monetary policy, inflation data, and external shocks are influencing financial markets across regions.

  3. 3

    EU financial markets still vulnerable to geopolitical uncertainty

    A recent analysis on EU financial markets reveals that geopolitical tensions continue to drive elevated volatility across equities, bonds, and crypto assets. The European Securities and Markets Authority (ESMA) report notes that tariff disputes, policy shifts, cyber threats, and technological vulnerabilities have kept risk levels high despite periodic market rebounds. This underscores how geopolitical instability remains a key force in shaping risk perceptions and financial market behavior in Europe.
